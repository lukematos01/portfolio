Lucas Matos

Hello! I'm Lucas Matos, a Brazil-based 2D artist dedicated to Visual Development. My heart lies in character design and concept art, and I've been lucky enough to turn that passion into a career since 2021.

Throughout my journey, I've collaborated on amazing projects for clients like Garena and Loot Studios. I'm also a mentor, and I love teaching and guiding students through online art mentorships!
